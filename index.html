02 · Clinical & Ward Tools

Equipment wayfinding and quiet bedside logging.

Shaped around hospital floor plans and night shifts, avoiding bright screens and basement Wi-Fi dead zones.

03 · Attention & Perception

Browser experiments on sight, focus, and reflection.

Small interactive pages using canvas motion, spatial models, and simple Web Audio tones to explore how we pay attention.

04 · Civic & Road Maintenance

Grouping duplicate street reports and logging road damage offline.

Practical municipal tools to stop dispatch teams sorting twenty reports for the same pothole.

05 · Cars, Safety & Records

Service histories that stay with the car, plus browser safety tests.

Including Datum Atelier, built to keep invoices and work records with the vehicle through every change of owner.
